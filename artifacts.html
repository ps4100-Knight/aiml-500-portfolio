PERSONAL REFLECTION

Innovation originates in fiction

Working on this project reinforced some of my pre-existing beliefs. Most innovative concepts in tech that are real today originated in someone’s imagination, someone’s desire. The person who first wished for or imagined a piece of technology may be just a writer, a philosopher, or some other kind of creative, but fiction inspires us to work towards ideas that were once just a piece of someone’s imagination. Innovation and invention are driven by our desire to turn fiction and fantasy into reality.
