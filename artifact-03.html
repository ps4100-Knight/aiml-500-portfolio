3.1 Adapting to Challenges

WRITTEN ASSIGNMENT
Pruthviraj SolankiIndiana Wesleyan UniversityAIML-500-01N1B: Machine Learning FundamentalsDeneen CollinsNovember 5, 2026

Introduction

This course assignment examines how I adapt and lead through a period of uncertainty.

Description

A personal written response about leadership, adapting to uncertainty, and making choices while navigating long-term challenges.

Objective

Consider how I lead through change, assess risks, seek guidance, and adjust my plans when the future is uncertain.

Process

Wrote an individual response drawing on family leadership, experience living abroad, faith, work, and lessons from people around me.

Tools & technologies used

Microsoft Word.

Value proposition

Unique value: shows a reflective, long-range approach to decision-making under uncertainty. Relevance: illustrates how adaptability and care for others can guide leadership through changing circumstances.

References

No outside references were listed in the assignment.

I believe my biggest leadership role in life right now is the one to lead my family, I am first one in a long bloodline to set foot outside and live in a foreign land. In a way I am the pioneer of my family as a friend once pointed out to me. One of the biggest challange I am facing right now is the American immigration system. I have already spent 6 years here and learned so much about the world, and its people by meeting a very diverse populus in this land of immigrants. At the same time, I face a challenge of wanting to go back home, wanting to meet those loved ones again in person and not through the lifeless pixels on a screen, what can I say even the mightiest warriors can tire in battles of attrition that go on too long, and they also long for the comforts of a safe place they can call home. The adaptation is this; I am going to give myself two more years here and see if I can make a stable home, a home where I can invite my family, friends and loved ones to live with. At the same time the employment economy volatile and so I am working on my own projects from which I can make something of my own to earn the right to stay here, rather than rely on a work visa that may require me to leave at some point due to factors not entirely under my control.

In terms of seeking wisdom of God, I have always been a curious one and in my curiosity, I have seen behind the curtains of many systems, religion being one of them. But just understanding the mechanics behind how a system works doesn't make the work of the system meaningless. There is wisdom in these religious scriptures, I look at the most religious two people in my life that are my parents, I look at the friends I have made who have accomplished great things in their lives in my opinion for their thoughts and perspectives, and I look at my own past experiences and mistakes to adapt and learn. My faith may not be following a religion as rules, but I do have faith in taking heed of the guidelines and wisdom of the scriptures and the ones I respect.

The risk I recently took was to decide if I want to go back to my home country and start building something there, or do I want to stay here in America where the hatred towards immigrants is brewing, the job stability is almost non-existent, the capitalist ideas exploit humans against my life philosophy of live and let live, a land where I miss my family and friends. I have seen the ones that came on this journey with me, my peers who were also from India during my Master's in Video game design at RIT. I have seen them spiral into negative thoughts to the point it has affected their health. Many of them have already returned to India in favor of their health. The constant stress is not worth the progress sometimes; in simpler words I have seen many friends go through what modern terms call "Burn out". I am afraid that with my timeline of two years I may face the same fate. However, I am working on multiple routes to gain experience and skills that will allow me to lead my family through this process. I am also guarding against Burn out by taking sufficient breaks and distracting myself from the stress through entertainment and spending time with friends.

3.3 AI Training Interaction

CONVERSATIONAL LEARNING
Pruthviraj SolankiIndiana Wesleyan UniversityAIML-500-01N1B: Machine Learning FundamentalsDeneen CollinsNovember 8, 2026

Introduction

This AI Training Interaction assignment uses storytelling and dialogue to explore machine learning concepts.

Description

A guided storytelling exercise about the development of computing and machine learning, paired with an AI-generated summary of what the model took from the exchange.

Objective

Explore machine learning concepts through dialogue, and examine how context, learned associations, and questioning affect an AI system’s explanations.

Process

I told a story connecting the growing need for computation to advances in computing technology and changing needs in modern society. I invited GPT-Astra to ask questions and challenge gaps; I responded to its questions, and the exchange shaped both my understanding and the model’s final summary.

Tools & technologies used

ChatGPT and Microsoft Word.

Value proposition

Unique value: demonstrates a conversational way to make abstract concepts approachable while questioning an AI’s assumptions. Relevance: helps learners see why plausible AI output still needs independent evaluation.

References

Shared learning conversation. The completion summary was generated with ChatGPT from my responses.
